5. Hypothesis testing and conclusion

H₀: the sample is drawn from a population following a normal distribution.

Skewness is −0.01, which is nearly zero, indicating a symmetrical distribution. The proximity of the mean (209.17), median (208.50) and mode (207.00) confirms this symmetry, and the histogram shows the bell shape typical of a Gaussian distribution.

Based on the visual evidence and the numerical parameters — low skewness and consistent measures of central tendency — we fail to reject H₀. The dataset follows the normal distribution law.
